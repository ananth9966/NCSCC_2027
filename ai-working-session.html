AI in TCU CS Programs Working Meeting

Special NCSCC Session — Friday, September 11, 2027

Overview

This working meeting provides a space for Tribal College and University representatives to discuss how artificial intelligence is being incorporated into computer science academic programs. Participants will share ideas, current practices, institutional needs, and plans related to teaching AI at TCUs.

This session is focused on TCU participants who have received AICF's CSI grant, and are strongly encouraged to attend both events.
