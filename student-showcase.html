Student Poster Competition

High school and college-level students are invited to present posters related to computing, cybersecurity, AI, and technology.

Abstract Submission

Students interested in participating must submit a short abstract. The abstract should describe the poster topic, project goal, approach, tools used, and expected outcomes or lessons learned.

Submit an Abstract

Important Dates

June 15, 2027 Submission Opens
August 23, 2027 Submission Closes

Two Competition Tracks

The poster competition will include separate tracks for college-level students and high school students. Each track will be reviewed separately so students are recognized within their academic level.

TRACK 1

College Poster Competition

For college-level students presenting research, capstone work, cybersecurity projects, software development, AI/ML, VR, or community-focused technology applications.

TRACK 2

High School Poster Competition

For high school students presenting STEM, computer science, cybersecurity, robotics, AI, app development, or other technology-related projects.
